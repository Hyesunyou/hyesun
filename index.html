About

Bio

Hyesun You is an Assistant Professor of Science Education in the Department of Teaching and Learning at the University of Iowa. She earned her Ph.D. in Science Education (2016) and an M.Ed. in Quantitative Methods with a psychometrics emphasis (2021) from the University of Texas at Austin, following an M.S. in Science Education and a B.S. in Chemistry (Biology minor) from Yonsei University, South Korea. Before entering the professoriate, she taught middle and high school science in Seoul and later held research and visiting-scholar positions at New York University, Michigan State University, and the University of Texas at Austin.

Her research develops and validates rigorous assessments of interdisciplinary science understanding, examines how technology-integrated instruction shapes K–12 teaching and learning, and applies machine learning and learning-analytics methods to large-scale datasets such as PISA. She currently serves as Co-Editor-in-Chief of Asia-Pacific Science Education and previously served as a Journal Board Member of the Journal of Research in Science Teaching (2024–2026), the top-ranked journal in science education.

Technology-Integrated Instructional Practices

How simulations, robotics, VR/AR, and digital tools shape science teaching and learning outcomes.

AI

AI Education in K–16

Generative AI and AI-personalized feedback for meaningful, interdisciplinary science learning.

D

Machine learning, topic modeling, and large-scale data (e.g., PISA) applied to science and math education.

With L. Zhu, J. Moon, T. Aldemir, H. Lee, D. Song, and S. Abu
In Preparation

AI-Enhanced Feedback as a Driver of Meaningful Learning in Carbon Cycling

Examining how AI-generated feedback (ISACC II + ChatGPT) supports interdisciplinary understanding of carbon cycling. Target: International Journal of Science Education.

With Z. Fang and L. Kauffman

A multi-year assessment-development program validating instruments for interdisciplinary science understanding of global carbon cycling, spanning IRT/Rasch modeling, topic modeling, and AI-feedback extensions.
